# Roadmap

## 1.0.0

Neardock identity, Windows and Android packaging, documentation, and product website.
Introduce the ink-and-orange interface, Chat and manual Clipboard while retaining the transfer core and existing file-sharing behaviour.

## Future development

Refine the Windows and Android experience and add separately scoped capabilities
on top of the existing core. Specific features will be scoped and tested in separate updates.
These are future intentions, not features advertised as available in 1.0.0.
iOS, macOS, Linux, Fire OS, CLI and app-store distribution are outside the current scope.
