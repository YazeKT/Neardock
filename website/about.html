Meet Neardock.

A practical home for sharing between Windows and Android.

A Yaze Media application

Designed and independently maintained by Yaze Media. Visit Yaze Media or follow Neardock’s changelog.

The product

Neardock shares files and messages between nearby devices. The first release brings an ink-and-orange interface, device conversations and manual clipboard sharing while preserving the existing transfer core.

What comes next

Future releases will build on the preserved transfer core. Additional features will be scoped and tested separately from the current Windows and Android review builds.

Open-source foundation

Neardock is based on LocalSend v1.18.2 by Tien Do Nam and contributors. It is independently maintained by Yaze Media and distributed under Apache 2.0. Upstream contributor credit and applicable notices are retained.
